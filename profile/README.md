We work with B2B founders in the messy middle, after product-market fit and before a repeatable go-to-market team. That's usually $1 million to $10 million in annual revenue. We publish the methods and tools we use with those founders because GTM needs more open source projects. Everything here is free to use and share.

Most of our work starts with an introduction from a fund. If a company you back comes to mind, [make an introduction](https://www.trellace.com/contact?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile&utm_campaign=trellace).

Platform teams can share anything here with portfolio companies. The playbooks are CC BY 4.0, so you can adapt them under your own name as long as you credit Trellace.
